# 给人类读者 / For Human Readers

## 1. 项目定位 / Project Vision

DH VR / V2 Research Version 是一个面向传统人文学术的长期研究项目。它以明清时期西学中文文献为核心材料，关注传教士、清廷官员、儒家士人、译者、润色者和知识机构之间的文本关系、思想关系与知识传播关系。

DH VR / V2 Research Version is a long-term humanities research project centered on Ming-Qing Chinese texts of Western Learning. It studies textual relations, intellectual relations, and knowledge transmission among missionaries, Qing officials, Confucian scholars, translators, revisers, and knowledge institutions.

本项目并不把数字技术视为目的，也不把网页、数据库或模型输出当作学术结论。它真正关心的是：传统文献学、思想史、知识史、宗教史与概念史研究，如何在大规模材料、复杂版本、跨文化术语和多重人物网络面前获得更稳定的材料控制能力。

This project does not treat digital technology as an end in itself. Nor does it treat web pages, databases, or model outputs as scholarly conclusions. Its central concern is how philology, intellectual history, history of knowledge, religious studies, and conceptual history can gain stronger control over large-scale, textually complex, and cross-cultural materials.

## 2. 核心材料 / Core Corpus

| 年代 / Year | 文献 / Text | 作者与相关人物 / Author and Related Figures | 学术意义 / Scholarly Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1674 | 《坤輿圖說》 / *Kunyu Tushuo* | 南怀仁 / Ferdinand Verbiest | 研究清初西方地理、天文和世界图像进入中文文本的重要材料。 / A key Chinese text for studying the transmission of geography, astronomy, and world knowledge in the early Qing. |
| 1799 | 《地球圖説》 / *Diqiu Tushuo* | 蒋友仁 / Michel Benoist；何国宗、钱大昕等相关人物 / related Qing revisers | 研究清廷语境下西学地理知识译述、润色与制度化表达的重要材料。 / A significant text for studying translation, revision, and institutionalization of geographical knowledge in a Qing court context. |

这两部文献只是入口。项目的长期目标，是整理和分析更大范围内的明清西学中文文献，包括传教士著述、清廷译述、士人序跋、地理天文文本、宗教论辩文本以及相关回应材料。

These two texts are only the entry point. The long-term goal is to curate and analyze a broader corpus of Ming-Qing Chinese texts of Western Learning, including missionary writings, Qing court translations, scholarly prefaces and postscripts, geographical and astronomical texts, religious polemics, and related Chinese responses.

## 3. 为什么重要 / Why This Project Matters

传统人文学术的根基仍然是材料、语境、证据链和解释。DH VR / V2 并不试图削弱这一点，而是试图加强它。

The foundation of humanities scholarship remains source materials, context, evidence, and interpretation. DH VR / V2 seeks to strengthen these foundations rather than replace them.

| 面向 / Dimension | 中文说明 | English Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 材料整理 / Source curation | 将分散文献、OCR 文本、校勘记录和来源说明统一管理。 | Organizes dispersed texts, OCR outputs, collation notes, and source records. |
| 文本细读 / Close reading | 通过段落编号、术语索引、KWIC 和证据卡片帮助研究者回到具体语境。 | Uses segment IDs, term indexes, KWIC, and evidence cards to return readers to textual contexts. |
| 概念史 / Conceptual history | 追踪术语的出现、变体、共现和解释方式。 | Tracks terms, variants, co-occurrences, and explanatory patterns. |
| 知识传播史 / History of knowledge | 比较同一知识命题在不同文本中的表达、改写和制度化。 | Compares how knowledge claims are expressed, rewritten, and institutionalized across texts. |
| 人物网络 / Network research | 连接传教士、士人、官员、机构和外部权威数据库。 | Links missionaries, literati, officials, institutions, and external authority databases. |
| 研究透明性 / Research transparency | 保留原始数据、清洗数据、抽取规则、人工复核状态和版本记录。 | Preserves raw data, cleaned data, extraction rules, review status, and version records. |
| 教学启发 / Pedagogical value | 为数字人文、思想史、宗教史和文献学训练提供可拆解案例。 | Offers a reusable case for digital humanities, intellectual history, religious studies, and philological training. |

本项目的基本信念是：数字方法不应替代人文学术判断，而应让学者更稳、更广、更细地掌握材料。

The guiding belief is that digital methods should not replace humanistic judgment. They should help scholars read more carefully, compare more systematically, and preserve evidence more responsibly.

## 4. 从 V1 到 VR / V2 / From V1 to VR / V2

本仓库最早源于作者参与 **“数字人文国际联合暑期工作坊 2023”** 的小组作业与过程文件。早期 V1 版本记录了对两部耶稣会士中文西学文本进行 OCR、人工清理、文献边界标注、CkipTagger 分词、CText N-gram 分析和初步比较的流程。

This repository began as part of the author's group work for the **2023 International Digital Humanities Summer Workshop**. The early V1 version documented a workflow for OCR, manual cleanup, book-boundary tagging, CkipTagger segmentation, CText N-gram analysis, and preliminary comparison of two Jesuit Chinese texts.

V1 的价值在于，它保留了项目最初形成时的实验过程和方法探索。VR / V2 并不是 V1 的简单增强版，而是把项目重新定位为一个可持续运行的明清西学文本与跨文化知识史研究平台。

V1 remains valuable as a record of the project's earliest experimental process and methodological exploration. VR / V2 is not a simple extension of V1; it reframes the project as a long-term research platform for Ming-Qing Western Learning texts and transcultural history of knowledge.

# 给人文学学生 / For Humanities Students

## 1. 如果你没有计算机背景，这个仓库能帮你做什么？ / What Can This Repository Do for You If You Do Not Have a Computer Science Background?

如果你是文学、历史、哲学、宗教学、艺术史、汉学或其他人文学专业的学生，这个仓库可以帮助你理解：数字人文不是把人文学术变成编程竞赛，而是用更稳定的方式保存材料、组织证据和提出问题。

If you are a student in literature, history, philosophy, religious studies, art history, Sinology, or another humanities field, this repository can help you understand that digital humanities is not about turning humanities research into a programming contest. It is about preserving sources, organizing evidence, and asking better questions in more stable ways.

你不需要先成为软件工程师，才可以从这个项目中学习。你可以把它当作一个开放案例，观察一项人文学研究如何从原始材料出发，经过 OCR、校勘、metadata、术语索引和证据页，逐步形成更可靠的研究判断。

You do not need to become a software engineer before learning from this project. You can treat it as an open case study showing how a humanities project moves from raw sources through OCR, collation, metadata, term indexing, and evidence pages toward more reliable scholarly interpretation.

## 2. 你可以学习什么？ / What Can You Learn?

| 能力 / Skill | 中文说明 | English Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 阅读原始材料 / Reading sources | 比较原文、OCR 文本和清洗文本，理解文本处理会影响解释。 | Compare original passages, OCR outputs, and cleaned texts, and see how text processing affects interpretation. |
| 建立元数据 / Building metadata | 记录题名、作者、年代、体裁、来源、OCR 状态和权利状态。 | Record title, author, date, genre, source, OCR status, and rights status. |
| 文献学判断 / Philological judgment | 判断哪些内容不能在清洗中删除，例如数字、音译词、官职、序跋。 | Decide what should not be deleted during cleaning, such as numbers, transliterated terms, official titles, and prefaces. |
| 概念史训练 / Conceptual history | 追踪 `地球`、`坤舆`、`赤道`、`经纬`、`五洲` 等术语。 | Track terms such as `地球`, `坤舆`, `赤道`, `经纬`, and `五洲`. |
| 证据写作 / Evidence-based writing | 学会让每个判断都回到一段具体文本证据。 | Learn to connect every interpretive claim to a concrete source passage. |
| 数字人文批判 / Critique of digital methods | 思考清洗、可视化和 AI 抽取会显示什么、遮蔽什么。 | Ask what data cleaning, visualization, and AI extraction reveal or hide. |
| 研究表达 / Research communication | 把一个研究问题整理为小型 digital essay 或 evidence page。 | Turn a research question into a small digital essay or evidence page. |

## 3. 一个适合初学者的小任务 / A Beginner-Friendly Exercise

从《坤輿圖說》或《地球圖説》中选择一段文字；记录它的 `document_id` 和 `segment_id`；选择一个术语，例如 `地球`、`坤舆`、`赤道` 或 `五洲`；做一个简单 KWIC 表；写出一个谨慎的观察，而不是夸大的结论；标出你仍然不确定的地方；最后检查机器输出是否真的符合原文。

Choose one passage from *Kunyu Tushuo* or *Diqiu Tushuo*; record its `document_id` and `segment_id`; choose one term, such as `地球`, `坤舆`, `赤道`, or `五洲`; make a simple KWIC table; write one cautious observation rather than an exaggerated conclusion; mark what remains uncertain; and finally check whether machine-generated output really matches the source text.

## 4. 一个简单原则 / A Simple Principle

如果一种数字方法能帮助你带着更好的问题回到原文，它就是有用的；如果它让你忘记原文，它就是危险的。

If a digital method helps you return to the source with better questions, it is useful. If it makes you forget the source, it is dangerous.

# 使用与复用 / Use and Reuse

后续研究者可以至少从四个方向复用本项目：第一，作为历史中文文本 corpus 建设范例；第二，作为文献学与数字人文结合的方法模板；第三，作为数字人文课程中的教学案例；第四，作为研究耶稣会士中文文本、清廷知识生产、中国基督教史和跨文化思想史的起点。

Later researchers may reuse this project in at least four ways: first, as a corpus-building model for historical Chinese texts; second, as a methodological template for combining philology and digital humanities; third, as a teaching case for digital humanities courses; and fourth, as a starting point for studying Jesuit Chinese texts, Qing court knowledge production, Chinese Christianity, and cross-cultural intellectual history.

任何复用都应保留原始证据、处理后数据、模型候选结果和人工复核解释之间的区别。

Any reuse should preserve the distinction between raw evidence, processed data, model-generated candidates, and human-verified interpretations.

# 授权与数据政策 / License and Data Policy

本仓库采用分层授权原则。项目自有代码和文档可以按仓库授权复用；原始文本具有来源差异，不应默认视为可自由再发布；外部 authority 数据应遵守原始数据库或平台的授权条件；自动抽取和模型生成结果在人工复核前应视为候选。

This repository follows a layered license policy. Project-created code and documentation may be reused under the repository license; raw texts are source-specific and should not be assumed to be freely redistributable; external authority data must follow the terms of the original databases or platforms; automatically extracted or model-generated results should be treated as candidates until human review.
